# A Complete Analysis of Twin Primes

**Author:** Xiangrui Wang  
**Version:** v1.4.2  
**Date:** September 11, 2026  
**Zenodo DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22701838

## Abstract

We give a self-contained exact representation of odd composite occupancy by the progressions `G_a(k)=a(a+2k)` and reduce Boolean occupancy to prime-base tracks. From this representation we derive prime-square activation, odd square-shell closure, modulo-6 window geometry, active-support classification, exact finite track intersections, finite-stage certification, and permanent stability of certified regions. For each window `(6n-1,6n+1)` we define a finite product `T_fin(n)` over the active primes and prove that it equals 1 exactly for an ordinary twin-prime pair. Writing `T={n in N:T_fin(n)=1}` and `N_twin(X)` for the cumulative twin-pair count, we prove the formal equivalence of `|T|=infinity`, continuation beyond every window threshold, unboundedness of `N_twin(X)`, and `lim_{X->infinity} N_twin(X)=infinity`. All universal results follow from exact integer arguments; the companion computations are independent regression audits.
