## Current architecture

For each integer \(N\ge2\), define

\[
A_N=3^N,
\qquad
I_N=(A_N,3A_N)=(3^N,3^{N+1}).
\]

The admissible research domain is the continuous shell chain

\[
I_N\to I_{N+1}\to I_{N+2}\to\cdots .
\]

The predecessor source set is

\[
O_N=\{a:3\le a<A_N,\ a\text{ odd}\}.
\]

Every actual projection into the current shell satisfies

\[
A_N<ab<3A_N,
\qquad b\ge3\text{ odd}.
\]

No primality prefilter is imposed on the sources. Composite, prime, and twin-prime classifications are assigned only after all actual product landings have been deduplicated.

## Projection completeness

The predecessor projection covers exactly all odd composite positions in the shell. Conversely, any odd composite \(x<3A_N\) has a nontrivial odd factor \(a<A_N\), so it is generated by a predecessor source.

Thus the residual odd positions after exact projection are precisely the prime positions in the shell.

## Exact window accounting

The complete six-window capacity is

\[
C_N=3^{N-1}.
\]

Let \(T_N,L_N,R_N,D_N\) be the counts of double-free, left-occupied, right-occupied, and double-occupied windows. Then

\[
C_N=T_N+L_N+R_N+D_N.
\]

With

\[
U_N=L_N+R_N+2D_N
\]

and \(Q_N\) the number of distinct interrupted windows,

\[
Q_N=U_N-D_N,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{T_N=C_N-Q_N=C_N-U_N+D_N}.
\]

Every double-free window corresponds exactly to a twin-prime pair in the shell.

## Scale-normalized transport

Writing \(x=a/A_N\), the shell-entry condition becomes

\[
1<xb<3.
\]

The normalized predecessor motion therefore has the same form at every shell scale.

## Permanent closure

For every future source \(a\ge A_N\) and nontrivial odd multiplier \(b\ge3\),

\[
ab\ge3A_N.
\]

Therefore a completed shell cannot be retroactively modified by sources born at or beyond its left boundary. The shell state is permanent after all predecessor projections are processed.

## Current load-bearing theorem

The final universal condition is

\[
\boxed{Q_N<C_N}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
T_N\ge1.
\]

If this holds for every admissible shell, or for infinitely many admissible shells, then permanent twin-prime windows accumulate across pairwise disjoint shells.

The present framework keeps the exact shell identities, projection completeness, closure theorem, computational evidence, and this universal non-full-coverage obligation logically separate.

## Review boundary

A valid counterexample to the strong shellwise statement must exhibit a complete admissible shell \(I_N=(3^N,3^{N+1})\) for which full predecessor projection and deduplication produce

\[
T_N=0.
\]

An arbitrary subinterval without a twin prime is not a counterexample to the complete-shell statement.

Finite computation remains an implementation check and counterexample search, not a substitute for a universal proof.
