# Research Roadmap

## Phase I — Public theorem freeze

- Freeze the exact W-MPOCCT statement and its positive-integer domain.
- Present RESET and ESCAPE with explicit selector policies.
- Record fixed-selector loss of control without a convergence inference.
- Maintain accurate researcher, institution, citation, and claims-boundary metadata.

## Phase II — Minimum-port program

- Resolve or refine MPD18.
- Resolve or refine PLDT.
- Extend exact \(\nu_3\)-layer reductions.
- Treat residue cylinders symbolically and label finite searches as finite.

## Phase III — Global mixed dynamics

- Study outward reachability from \(3\).
- Test and characterize mixed strong connectivity.
- Investigate universal and \(3\)-anchored cycle embedding.

Phase III is not established by Phase I. Local control-option completeness is not global reachability, strong connectivity, or a proof of the classical Collatz conjecture.
